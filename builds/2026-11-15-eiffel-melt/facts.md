## Labelled as not literal
- The melt is a stylized simulation: the tower melts from the top down and the time is sped up (chip: "SIMULATION · STYLIZED · SPED UP"). The temperature/height gauge is driven by the animation, not by a heat model.
- The cube and the sheet are drawn to scale (9.75 m; 125 m × 5.93 cm) (chip: "THE REAL AMOUNT OF IRON · TO SCALE").
- The ghost tower and wind streaks are a diagram (chip: "DIAGRAM").
- The tower model is stylized from the official figures (legs 25 m wide on a 125 m square, floors at 57 m, 115.7 m and 276 m, 330 m to the antenna tip). The standing figure next to the cube is 1.75 m, for scale.

## Left out
- "The tower grows up to 15 cm in summer": common online, but not on the official pages read this run.
- Any figure for the tower's sway in the wind, the 60 tonnes of paint (true, press kit p.7, but it didn't fit the story), and "the air around it weighs more than the tower" (depends on the shape you draw around it).
- A 6 cm "melted down" claim attributed to the official site: it isn't on the pages read; the 6 cm here is our own math from the official figures.
