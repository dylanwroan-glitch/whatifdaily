# If the Sun were a door, where's Earth? — fact sheet

Format: scale shock. The model shrinks the Sun to the height of a front door (2 m) and keeps every other size and distance in proportion.
Quotes below were read from the NASA pages on 2026-11-10.

## Scale math (labelled "SCALE MATH · NASA DATA" on screen)

Scale: Sun diameter 1,392,000 km = 2.0 m door, so 1 m in the model = 696,000 km.

| On screen | Math |
|---|---|
| Sun = **2 m** door (the premise) | NASA's analogy uses "a typical front door"; the video uses 2 m (a standard US front door is 80 in = 2.03 m). |
| Earth: **1.8 cm** ball (shown, not captioned) | 12,756 / 1,392,000 × 2 m = 0.0183 m. A US nickel is 21.2 mm across, matching NASA's "size of a nickel". |
| Earth sits **215 m** away | 150,000,000 / 1,392,000 × 2 m = 215.5 m (93,000,000 mi / 864,000 mi × 2 m = 215.3 m). |
| From there, the door-Sun looks as big as the real Sun | Angular size is unchanged by scaling: 2 m at 215 m = 0.53°, the same as the real Sun seen from Earth (1,392,000 km at 150,000,000 km). Logic, no new number on screen. |
| Neptune: **6.5 km** away | 30 × 215.4 m = 6,463 m (2.8 billion mi / 864,000 mi × 2 m = 6,481 m). |
| Proxima Centauri: **57,600 km** away | 4.24 × 9.4607 × 10^12 km = 4.011 × 10^13 km; / 1,392,000 km × 2 m = 57,630 km. With NASA's rounded "9.5 trillion km" it is 57,870 km. Shown as 57,600 km (the counter rounds 57,630 to the nearest 100). |
| **1.4 times** around the whole Earth | Earth's circumference = π × 12,756 km = 40,074 km; 57,630 / 40,074 = 1.44. |

Not numbers (illustration): the porch, the street, the town and the flying Earth are scenery. The 1.3 million counter is a visual count-up to NASA's figure, not one sphere per Earth. The globe's continents are hand-drawn and simplified.
